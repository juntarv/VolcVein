Three rules
of the rift
Skip

1 · Shut, never steer

Tap a valve and that branch closes. Pressure has to go somewhere, so everything upstream doubles down the branches you left open.

2 · Shut veins crust

A closed branch cools. After a few seconds it seals for good — unless you press and hold to blow the crust out, which costs a bite of chamber pressure.

3 · Fill the rim molds

Every vent ends in a stone mold. Fill them all and the rift casts an obsidian piece. Flood one and it cracks; starve one and it sets solid.
